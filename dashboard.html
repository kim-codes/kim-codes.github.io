Program Pulse
Reading a program's vitals

Program updates have a way of multiplying. A milestone here, a risk there, a dozen status updates in between... Eventually everything is scattered and that's when leadership wants an update ASAP.

I built Program Pulse to explore two things: how I would bring those signals together to tell the full story of a program, and how AI could become part of my toolkit along the way.
