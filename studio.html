Key Takeaways

01

Search isn't always about words.

Vector search looks beyond exact matches and finds ideas that are close in meaning.

02

Meaning can become coordinates.

Embeddings transform ideas into numbers that represent their position in a larger space.

03

The galaxy doesn't know friend from foe.

Vector search finds similarity, but humans decide what the results mean.
